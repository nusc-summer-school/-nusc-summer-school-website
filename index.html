About Our Summer School

The NUSC Summer School offers intensive, hands-on training in cutting-edge computational methods and technologies. Our program is designed for students, researchers, and professionals who want to enhance their skills and stay at the forefront of their fields.

Hands-on Learning

All workshops include practical, hands-on sessions where you'll apply what you've learned to real-world problems.

Expert Instructors

Learn from leading experts in academia and industry who are passionate about sharing their knowledge.

Networking Opportunities

Connect with peers and instructors from around the world to build your professional network.

Frequently Asked Questions

What prior knowledge is required for the workshops?

Most workshops are designed for participants with a basic understanding of programming concepts. Specific prerequisites for each workshop are listed in the workshop descriptions. Beginners are welcome to join the introductory workshops, while more advanced workshops may require specific prior knowledge.

Is there accommodation available for participants?

Yes, we have arrangements with nearby hotels and university dormitories for discounted accommodation rates. Details will be provided after registration. Participants are responsible for making their own reservations.

What should I bring to the workshops?

Participants should bring their own laptops with the required software pre-installed. Installation instructions will be sent to registered participants two weeks before the start of the summer school. All necessary datasets and materials will be provided.

Are there any scholarships available?

Yes, we offer a limited number of scholarships for students and early-career researchers. The application process for scholarships is separate from the regular registration. Please check the scholarship section on our website for more information and deadlines.

Will I receive a certificate after completing the workshops?

Yes, all participants who attend the full duration of their registered workshops will receive a certificate of completion. Additionally, participants who successfully complete the team projects will receive a project-based certificate.
